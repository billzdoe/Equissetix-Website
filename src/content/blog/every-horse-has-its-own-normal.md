# Every Horse Has Its Own Normal

Textbooks put an adult horse's resting temperature at about 99 to 101 °F and resting pulse at about 28 to 44 beats a minute. Those ranges are right for horses in general. They're not right for your horse in particular.

A horse that usually runs at 99.0 °F and reads 100.6 °F this morning is inside the textbook range. He's also 1.6 degrees above his own normal, and that's worth a look. TrainingTree Pro is built to catch exactly that.

## How it works

Every temperature, pulse and respiration reading goes into the horse's record, whether it comes from a sensor, a barn walk or a thermometer read on the shed row. From those readings TrainingTree Pro learns each horse's **own normal**, then checks every new reading against it. A rise in temperature or resting pulse that's meaningful for that horse is flagged, and a quick second reading confirms it before anyone is called out.

A horse without enough readings yet isn't flagged against a guess; the app learns its normal first.

## Morning rounds, rebuilt

The **Rounds** view lists every horse in care in stall order. Each one shows today's readings against its own normal, what has and hasn't been checked today, and who needs a look first. Exceptions rise to the top.

Taking readings is quick: walk the shed row and enter them on a keypad as you go. If the barn's signal drops, readings wait safely on the phone, marked as "waiting to send", and go through the moment the signal returns, stored once even if they're sent twice.

Every flag says where its reading came from (a hand reading, a sensor or a barn walk), so the vet knows exactly what they're looking at.

## Why it matters

- **Changes that matter for this horse.** A rise against the horse's own normal is flagged even when the reading is still inside a textbook range.
- **A sensible baseline for every horse.** A horse who always runs a little warm is compared with his own normal, not with an average.
- **A history the vet can use.** Weeks of readings, already organised, instead of a phone call that starts with "he's been a bit off."

## The rest of the health file

Baselines are one part of a complete health record in TrainingTree Pro and StableTree:

- Vet examinations from pre-purchase to emergency, with flexion, cardiac, respiratory and dental findings.
- Vaccinations and deworming on separate schedules with their own reminders.
- Henneke body-condition scores with photos, charted against the healthy 4–6 range.
- Medication logs with withdrawal periods.
- Rehab plans with pain, mobility and strength tracked over time.

Every horse is different. Now their records treat them that way.
